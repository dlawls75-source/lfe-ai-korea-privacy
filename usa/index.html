1. General Privacy Principles

LFE AI USA does not provide an app-specific account or login system. The developer does not operate a member database that directly collects and stores users' names, email addresses, phone numbers, or street addresses through the app.

2. Information That Third-Party Services May Process

The app may use third-party services such as Google AdMob for advertising, consent management, fraud prevention, measurement, diagnostics, and performance. Depending on the user's device settings, region, consent status, and the third-party provider's practices, these services may process information such as:

  • Approximate location: for example, country or region inferred from an IP address.
  • Device identifiers: advertising or other device-level identifiers when permitted by the platform and user settings.
  • Product interaction: app usage and interaction information.
  • Advertising data: ad impressions and interactions.
  • Crash and diagnostic data: information used to identify errors and improve stability.
  • Performance data: information related to app responsiveness and operation.
  • Other diagnostic data: additional SDK diagnostic information used to maintain reliability, diagnose issues, measure service operation, and support advertising or analytics functions.

Actual processing is controlled by applicable platform settings, regional requirements, consent choices, and third-party service policies.

3. Advertising and Consent

Free users may be shown ads through Google AdMob. The app uses consent readiness before requesting ads where required. Advertising services may process device, approximate location, advertising, interaction, diagnostic, and performance information for ad delivery, fraud prevention, measurement, and related purposes.

The current iOS build does not request App Tracking Transparency permission and does not use app data for tracking users across apps or websites owned by other companies. Apple SKAdNetwork may be used for privacy-preserving advertising attribution.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

4. In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

LFE AI USA may offer LFE PRO through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Store payment systems process purchases. The developer does not directly collect or store full payment-card or bank-account details.

Store purchase information may be checked to determine whether LFE PRO access is active. If a secure server-verification service is configured in the future, only purchase-verification information required for entitlement checking would be transmitted to that service.

5. Camera, Ticket Scanning, and OCR

The Scan Ticket feature requests camera access only when the user opens the scanner. The camera is used to capture printed Powerball or Mega Millions number areas for text recognition.

Ticket OCR is performed on the device using a bundled text-recognition model. The captured ticket image and recognized text are not uploaded by the app for OCR and are not sent to advertising code as ticket content.

The captured image is used as temporary cache input and is deleted after processing, retaking, discarding, or leaving the scanning flow when cleanup succeeds. The app does not intentionally save ticket photos to the user's photo library.

Scanning is an assistance feature only. Users should review recognized numbers and verify any prize with an official lottery source.

6. Saved Picks and App Settings

Saved number combinations, preferences, and related app settings may be stored locally on the device so the app can provide its features. These local records are not stored in a developer-operated member account database.

7. Draw and Result Data

The app retrieves Powerball and Mega Millions winning-number data from New York State Open Data. Network requests to that public-data service may reveal ordinary connection information such as an IP address. The app may also use embedded verified draw history as a fallback when appropriate.

Jackpot and jackpot-history information may be retrieved from publicly accessible lottery web services. Those services receive ordinary connection information such as an IP address. LFE AI USA remains an independent app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by an official lottery organization.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

The developer does not maintain a separate member database of directly collected personal information for this app. Locally stored picks and settings can be removed through available app controls or by deleting the app. Data processed by third-party services is retained and deleted according to those providers' policies.

9. Children's Privacy

The app does not provide a child-focused account-registration system and does not intentionally request children to submit direct personal information to the developer. Platform and advertising restrictions may apply based on region and age.

10. International Processing

Global service providers such as Google, Apple, and Google Play may process data outside the user's state or country in accordance with their policies and applicable law.
